## Job Description Snapshot

Figma builds collaborative design and prototyping tools used by designers and engineers worldwide. Engineering interns work on the core product (canvas rendering, real-time collaboration, plugins API, FigJam) and internal tooling.

**Typical requirements:**
- Pursuing a BS/MS in CS or related field
- Strong CS fundamentals (data structures, algorithms, systems)
- Experience with JavaScript/TypeScript or C++; frontend or rendering experience a plus
- Interest in product-driven engineering and user-facing features

**What interns do:**
- Build features shipped to millions of Figma users
- Work in a small team with high ownership
- Collaborate with designers and PMs on UX-driven engineering problems
