## JD Snapshot

Samsara builds a connected operations platform for industrial IoT — fleet telematics, video safety, and equipment monitoring at scale. SWE interns work on backend services and cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP) that ingest and process billions of sensor events per day. The role emphasizes distributed systems, reliability, and the ability to ship features end-to-end.

**Key requirements:**
- Proficiency in Python, Go, or Java
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS or GCP)
- Understanding of distributed systems and APIs
- Bonus: IoT, time-series data, or real-time streaming experience
- Enthusiasm for physical operations and industrial tech

**Keywords:** AWS, GCP, cloud infrastructure, IoT backend, distributed systems, backend engineering, reliability
