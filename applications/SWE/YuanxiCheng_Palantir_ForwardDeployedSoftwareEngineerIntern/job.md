## Role Summary

Palantir's Forward Deployed Software Engineer (FDSE) role is a hybrid of software engineering and technical consulting. FDSEs are embedded at client sites (government, healthcare, finance, defense), build custom integrations and features on top of Palantir's platforms (Gotham, Foundry, AIP), and translate client requirements into working software. The role demands both strong coding ability and the communication skills to work directly with stakeholders. Interns in this track are evaluated for full-time FDSE offers.
