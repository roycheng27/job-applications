## Job Description Snapshot

Citadel is one of the world's leading alternative investment management firms. They seek Software Engineer Interns to build high-performance trading and risk systems.

**Key Qualifications:**
- Enrolled in a BS/MS/PhD in Computer Science, Engineering, or Mathematics
- Graduating 2027 or 2028
- Strong command of C++ or Python
- Deep knowledge of data structures, algorithms, and systems design
- Demonstrated ability to write clean, efficient code

**Preferred:**
- Interest in financial systems and low-latency applications
- Experience with NLP, machine learning, or quantitative methods
- Strong mathematical background
- Experience with large-scale data systems

**Responsibilities:**
- Design and develop software for trading, risk, and research systems
- Build high-performance, scalable components of Citadel's technology stack
- Work with traders and quants to translate quantitative research into production systems
- Optimize systems for performance and reliability
