Datadog is the cloud-native observability and monitoring platform used by thousands of engineering organizations. Software Engineering interns work on core product teams building distributed backend infrastructure, agent systems, and data pipelines that process billions of events per second.

**Responsibilities:**

- Develop production-quality code that handles massive scale
- Collaborate with full-time engineers on real customer-facing systems

**Requirements / Preferred Qualifications:**
- Pursuing BS/MS in Computer Science or related field
- Strong programming skills (Python, Go, Java, or C++)
- Understanding of distributed systems, scalability, and reliability
- Interest in cloud-native infrastructure, observability, or backend systems
- Experience with data pipelines or production engineering is a plus
