## Job Description Snapshot

Datadog is the cloud-scale monitoring and analytics platform used by DevOps, SRE, and security teams. Engineering interns join product teams building the Agent, APM, Logs, Infrastructure, or Security products.

**Typical requirements:**
- Pursuing a BS/MS in CS or related field
- Strong understanding of data structures, algorithms, and systems
- Experience with Go, Python, or Java; distributed systems or observability background a plus
- Cloud infrastructure experience (AWS, GCP, Azure) valued

**What interns do:**
- Build production features in Datadog's monitoring platform
- Work on reliability-critical systems with high throughput requirements
- Collaborate with senior engineers in a fast-moving engineering org
