## Role Summary

Datadog is the cloud observability and monitoring platform used by DevOps and infrastructure teams worldwide. SWE interns work on product teams building features across Datadog's suite — metrics ingestion, distributed tracing, log management, dashboards, alerting, or integrations with cloud providers (AWS, GCP, Azure). The tech stack is primarily Go and Python, with a heavy emphasis on distributed systems, high-throughput data pipelines, and backend engineering. Interns ship real features to production.
