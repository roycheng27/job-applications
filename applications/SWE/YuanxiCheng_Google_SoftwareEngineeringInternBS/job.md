## Role Summary

Google Software Engineering Intern (BS) is a broadly scoped role across Google's product and infrastructure teams. Interns design, develop, test, and deploy software, contributing to real product features. Key attributes valued:

- Strong CS fundamentals: data structures, algorithms, systems
- Proficiency in one or more of: C++, Java, Python, Go
- Clean, well-tested, maintainable code
- Ability to work across large codebases and collaborate with cross-functional teams
- Experience with ML/AI a plus for relevant teams
