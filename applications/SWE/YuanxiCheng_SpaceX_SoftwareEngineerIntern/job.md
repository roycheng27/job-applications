## Job Description Summary

SpaceX's Fleet Health Instrumentation team builds systems to monitor, diagnose, and ensure the reliability of SpaceX's rocket and satellite fleet. Software Engineer interns work on telemetry ingestion pipelines, real-time monitoring dashboards, anomaly detection systems, and infrastructure that keeps Starship and Starlink operating safely.

**Responsibilities:**
- Build data pipelines to ingest and process high-volume telemetry from rockets and satellites
- Develop monitoring and alerting systems to surface fleet health anomalies
- Work with time-series data and event-driven architectures
- Collaborate with vehicle and launch operations teams

**Requirements / Preferred Qualifications:**
- Pursuing BS/MS in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field
- Strong Python and/or C++ programming skills
- Experience with data pipelines, ETL systems, or real-time processing
- Interest in systems reliability, instrumentation, or embedded environments
- SQL/database skills for querying operational data
