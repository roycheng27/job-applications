## Job Description Snapshot

Uber is hiring new-grad Software Engineers to work on its core platform and product systems. Engineers own features end-to-end, working with large-scale distributed systems, microservices, and high-throughput data pipelines.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Design, develop, and deploy scalable backend services
- Collaborate cross-functionally with PMs, designers, and other engineers
- Participate in code reviews and maintain high engineering standards
- Contribute to reliability and observability of production systems

**Requirements:**
- BS/MS in Computer Science, Statistics, or related field (graduated or graduating by summer 2027)
- Proficiency in one or more of: Python, Java, Go, C++
- Understanding of data structures, algorithms, and distributed systems
- Experience building and deploying production software
