## Job Description Snapshot

Palantir's Forward Deployed Software Engineer (FDSE) role embeds engineers directly with clients (government, enterprise, healthcare) to deploy and extend the Gotham/Foundry/AIP platforms. Interns work alongside FDSEs on client deployments, data integration, and custom application development.

**Typical requirements:**
- Pursuing a BS in CS or related technical field
- Strong software engineering fundamentals
- Comfort working with messy, real-world data and ambiguous problem statements
- Excellent communication skills; ability to present technical work to non-technical stakeholders
- Python, Java, or similar; SQL a strong plus

**What interns do:**
- Integrate client data sources into Palantir Foundry pipelines
- Build custom ontologies and applications on top of the Palantir platform
- Present findings and demo solutions directly to client stakeholders
