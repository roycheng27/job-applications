## JD Snapshot

Optiver is a global market maker and prop trading firm known for mathematically-rigorous, technology-first trading strategies. SWE interns build production systems and tools that support options trading, risk management, and market-making algorithms in high-throughput, latency-sensitive environments.

**Responsibilities:**
- Design and build tools and infrastructure for trading systems and market-making algorithms
- Optimize software for correctness, performance, and low latency
- Work in C++ and Python on production financial software
- Collaborate with traders and researchers on algorithmic problems

**Requirements / Preferred:**
- Strong proficiency in C++ and/or Python
- Strong mathematical and algorithmic reasoning (probability, optimization, combinatorics)
- Understanding of computer architecture, OS, networking, or systems programming
- Interest in financial markets and technology-driven trading
- BS/MS in CS, Math, Physics, or related; graduating 2027+

**Keywords:** C++, Python, algorithms, mathematical modeling, financial systems, high-performance, optimization, probability, market-making
