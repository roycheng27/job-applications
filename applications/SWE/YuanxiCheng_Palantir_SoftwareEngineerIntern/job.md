## JD Snapshot

Palantir builds software platforms (Foundry, AIP, Gotham, Apollo) used by governments, hospitals, and enterprises to integrate, manage, and analyze large-scale data. Interns work as full engineers on Palantir's forward deployed or product teams.

**Responsibilities:**
- Design and build user-facing features and back-end services at production quality
- Collaborate directly with customers and PMs to shape requirements
- Write clean, scalable code across the full stack (front-end, back-end, infrastructure)
- Participate in on-call rotations and own your code in production

**Requirements / Preferred:**
- Proficiency in at least one of: Java, Python, C++, Go
- Strong understanding of data structures and algorithms
- Experience with distributed systems or cloud infrastructure is a plus
- Eligibility and willingness to obtain a US security clearance (noted; not auto-disqualifying)
- Pursuing BS/MS in CS, Math, Stats, or related; graduating 2027 or later

**Keywords:** distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, Python, Java, data structures, scalable applications, production code, user-facing software, full-stack
