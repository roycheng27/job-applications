## JD Snapshot

Google's Software Engineer Intern program places students on product or infrastructure teams across Google Cloud, YouTube, Search, Android, and more. Interns design, code, and test substantial features on real systems. The role emphasizes strong CS fundamentals — data structures, algorithms, and systems — along with proficiency in C++ or Python.

**Key requirements:**
- Currently pursuing BS/MS in Computer Science or related field
- Strong knowledge of data structures and algorithms
- Proficiency in C++, Java, or Python
- Experience with distributed systems or large-scale infrastructure a plus
- GPA 3.5+ preferred; strong coding interview performance required

**Keywords:** C++, data structures, algorithms, systems, distributed, scalability, software engineering fundamentals
