## Job Description Snapshot

Google's Software Engineer Intern program places undergrad students on product teams across Search, Cloud, Maps, YouTube, Android, and more. Interns work on real projects under mentor guidance with full engineering team integration.

**Typical requirements:**
- Pursuing a BS in Computer Science, Statistics, or related technical field (not yet graduated)
- Strong fundamentals in data structures, algorithms, and software design
- Experience with one or more general-purpose programming languages (Python, Java, C++, Go)
- Ability to design, implement, test, and iterate on software components

**What interns do:**
- Design, develop, and test software components for Google products
- Collaborate with full-time engineers and product managers
- Present findings and demos at project milestones
