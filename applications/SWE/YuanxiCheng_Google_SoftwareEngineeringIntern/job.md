Google's Software Engineering Intern (BS) role is a 12-14 week program where interns work on a real product team alongside full-time engineers. Projects vary by host team but typically involve full-stack development, systems infrastructure, ML infrastructure, or data platform work. Google heavily uses C++ and Java internally.

**Key responsibilities:**
- Design, implement, and ship software that affects products serving billions of users
- Work within an Agile/iterative development team with code review and testing standards
- Contribute to large-scale codebases with an emphasis on correctness, performance, and maintainability
- Collaborate with engineers, PMs, and UX designers

**Key qualifications (from Google careers page):**
- Currently pursuing a BS in CS or related field; graduating 2027 or 2028 preferred
