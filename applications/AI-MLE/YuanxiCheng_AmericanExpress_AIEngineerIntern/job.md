## Job Description Snapshot

American Express's AI/ML team builds generative AI and LLM-powered products for customer service, fraud detection, and marketing personalization. The AI Engineer Intern helps design and evaluate agentic and RAG-based AI systems.

**Key responsibilities:**
- Build and evaluate LLM-based agentic workflows (RAG, tool use, prompt engineering)
- Implement embedding and vector retrieval systems for organizational knowledge
- Work with NLP/transformer models and responsible AI evaluation frameworks
- Collaborate with product and engineering teams to ship AI features

**Requirements:**
- Experience with Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, LangChain or similar LLM frameworks
- Familiarity with embeddings, vector databases, retrieval-augmented generation
- Understanding of responsible AI practices and bias evaluation
- Graduating 2025–2027; US work authorization (sponsorship available for some roles)
