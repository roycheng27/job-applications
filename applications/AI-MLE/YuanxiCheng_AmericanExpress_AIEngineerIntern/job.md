## JD Snapshot

American Express is building enterprise AI capabilities to personalize customer experiences, detect fraud, and automate financial workflows. The AI Engineer Intern role focuses on integrating LLM-based pipelines and AI-assisted retrieval systems into AmEx's Java-heavy tech stack. Interns work on prompt engineering, RAG pipelines, and model evaluation in an enterprise financial services context.

**Key requirements:**
- Proficiency in Python and Java (enterprise Java stack)
- Experience with LLMs, RAG, or AI-assisted retrieval
- Familiarity with vector databases and embedding models
- Understanding of responsible AI and data governance
- Strong communication skills for cross-functional collaboration

**Keywords:** Java, enterprise AI, AI-assisted retrieval, LLM integration, RAG, financial services, responsible AI
