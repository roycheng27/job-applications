## JD Snapshot

ByteDance's Applied ML team builds and maintains production ML infrastructure that serves billions of users across TikTok, Douyin, and other products. The Production Engineer Intern role bridges model development and deployment — interns work on training pipelines, serving infrastructure, and optimization for large-scale ML systems running on GCP/cloud infrastructure.

**Key requirements:**
- Experience with ML production pipelines and deployment
- Proficiency in Python; C++ or Go a plus
- Cloud infrastructure experience (GCP, AWS preferred)
- Familiarity with distributed training and model serving
- Understanding of model optimization (quantization, distillation, latency tradeoffs)

**Keywords:** production deployment, ML system, GCP, cloud infrastructure, training pipelines, model serving, distributed ML
