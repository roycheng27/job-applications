## Job Description Snapshot

Castleton Commodities International (CCI) is a leading global commodity trading firm. They seek Data Science / ML interns to build quantitative and predictive models supporting trading and risk operations.

**Key Qualifications:**
- Pursuing a BS/MS in Statistics, Data Science, Computer Science, or related quantitative field
- Expected graduation Winter 2027 or Spring/Summer 2028 (undergrad eligible)
- Strong Python programming skills
- Experience with ML frameworks (scikit-learn, XGBoost, PyTorch/TensorFlow)
- Proficiency in data manipulation (pandas, NumPy, SQL)
- Strong statistics/probability background
- Ability to work with large datasets and build predictive models

**Preferred:**
- Experience with time series forecasting or financial data
- Familiarity with feature engineering for tabular data
- Exposure to commodities markets or quantitative finance

**Responsibilities:**
- Develop and validate machine learning models for trading signals or risk assessment
- Engineer features from structured and unstructured data sources
- Analyze large datasets to surface actionable insights for trading desks
- Collaborate with quants and data engineers on production model pipelines
