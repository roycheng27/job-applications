## Role Summary

Microsoft Turing is the applied research team behind large-scale NLP and LLM products (Copilot,
Bing AI, Office AI). The Applied Scientist Intern works on deep learning research and engineering
for language models, including pretraining, fine-tuning, RLHF, and evaluation.
