## Job Description Snapshot

The Nuclear Company is building a fleet-based approach to nuclear energy deployment to accelerate the US clean energy transition. This AI Applied Research Intern role supports ML/AI research applied to nuclear plant operations, permitting acceleration, supply chain optimization, or safety analysis.

**Typical requirements:**
- Pursuing a BS/MS in CS, Statistics, Data Science, or related field
- Experience with machine learning, statistical modeling, or applied AI research
- Python proficiency; familiarity with scikit-learn, PyTorch, or similar ML libraries
- Ability to work independently on research problems with ambiguous requirements
- **US Person requirement:** US citizens, permanent residents, asylees, or refugees only

**What interns do:**
- Apply ML/statistical methods to nuclear operations or regulatory data
- Build and evaluate models; document findings for technical and non-technical audiences
- Collaborate with engineers and domain experts in a fast-moving startup environment
