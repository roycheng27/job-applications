## Job Description (inferred via web search)

Capital One seeks a Data Analyst Intern to support business intelligence and data-driven decision making across its technology and financial services lines. The role involves working with large datasets to surface insights, build dashboards, and support analytical initiatives.

**Key responsibilities:**
- Query and analyze large datasets using SQL and Python to support business decisions
- Build and maintain dashboards and visualizations (Tableau, Power BI) for stakeholder reporting
- Conduct exploratory data analysis to identify trends, patterns, and actionable insights
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define metrics and KPIs
- Present findings to managers and business partners

**Qualifications:**
- Currently enrolled in a quantitative degree program (Statistics, CS, Math, Economics)
- Strong SQL skills; Python or R experience a plus
- Experience with BI tools (Tableau preferred)
- Strong communication skills; ability to translate data findings for non-technical stakeholders
