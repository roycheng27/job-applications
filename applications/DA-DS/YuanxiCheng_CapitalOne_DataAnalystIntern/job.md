## JD Snapshot

Capital One is a technology-forward bank that uses data extensively across credit, marketing, operations, and risk. Data Analyst Interns work alongside business analysts and data scientists to surface actionable insights from large datasets, build dashboards, and support data-driven decision-making within a business unit.

**Responsibilities:**
- Query and analyze large datasets using SQL and Python to answer business questions
- Build and maintain dashboards and visualizations (Tableau, Excel, or similar)
- Present findings to stakeholders and translate data into business recommendations
- Collaborate with product, engineering, and strategy teams on analytical projects

**Requirements / Preferred:**
- Proficiency in SQL; Python a strong plus
- Experience with BI tools (Tableau, Power BI, or similar) preferred
- Strong analytical and communication skills
- Coursework in statistics, data analysis, or quantitative methods
- BS/MS in Statistics, Math, CS, Economics, or related; graduating 2027+
- **Note:** This role does not offer visa sponsorship

**Keywords:** SQL, Python, data analysis, dashboards, Tableau, stakeholder communication, business insights, Excel, statistical analysis
