## JD Snapshot

Capital One's Data Analyst Intern program places students on business intelligence, risk, or product analytics teams. Interns write SQL queries, build Tableau dashboards, and present data-driven recommendations to senior stakeholders. Capital One uses BigQuery and Snowflake on GCP/AWS; interns with Python and dplyr skills can accelerate analysis workflows.

**Key requirements:**
- Proficiency in SQL for data querying and manipulation
- Experience with data visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI)
- Python or R for statistical analysis
- Ability to communicate findings clearly to non-technical stakeholders
- Coursework in statistics, data analysis, or business analytics

**Keywords:** SQL, data analysis, Tableau, stakeholder reporting, BigQuery, Snowflake, business intelligence, financial services
