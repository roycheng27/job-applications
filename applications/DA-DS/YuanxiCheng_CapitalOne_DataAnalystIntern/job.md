## Job Description Snapshot

Capital One's Data Analyst Intern roles sit within business units (credit card, auto finance, enterprise data) and focus on SQL-based analysis, dashboard development, and translating data into business decisions for senior stakeholders.

**Key responsibilities:**
- Write SQL queries to analyze large datasets and extract business insights
- Build and maintain Tableau/Excel dashboards for recurring reporting
- Communicate findings to stakeholders and support business decisions with data
- Collaborate with data engineers and business partners on data quality and definitions

**Requirements:**
- Strong SQL skills; Python a plus
- Experience with Tableau, Excel, or similar BI tools
- Ability to communicate data findings to non-technical audiences
- Graduating 2025–2027
